# Dwarven summoned citadel (`DwarvenSummonedCitadelKeep`)

Model `DBCitadel`, mesh `TOWER` (the three tower heads, the tower plinth and the gate), own texture
`DBFortressK.tga` (from the faction atlas `DBFortress1.tga`). `Tier.STANDARD`. The rock (`STONEA`),
the three statues and the door are EA's. It takes the fortress's language so the two read as one family.

## What changed

- **Keep** (the tall octagon): a crown ring over the rune band, stepped pyramids on the flat sides,
  stepped gables on the long chamfers. The bronze disc is kept.
- **Shield tower**: the fortress's tower head and crown lowered onto the shields, stepped gables
  over them, a gilded stepped pinnacle on the cap.
- **Round tower**: the keep's crown ring and a stepped gable on each side.
- **Gate**: a deep stepped pointed portal round EA's opening, run back into the rock; a rune
  lintel, bronze cornice and stepped gable over it.
- **Banners**: two poles either side of the gate; cloth in `DBHCCitadel`.

## Kept clear

- EA's bollards in front of the gate.
- The flame add-on [`fortress_braziers`](../fortress/README.md) (`DBFFlam`), drawn here too at
  x 80.8..90.4 beside the right statue.
