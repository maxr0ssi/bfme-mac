## What changed

  1.55 apart, a rounded rail 2.5 over the treads with gilt caps at its ends.
- **Lanterns**: a lantern column (fluted shaft, gilt leaf capital, crystal lantern) at both corners
  of each flight's foot, a taller one on the landing where they part.
- **Paint**: the slabs in ivory stone; the shared sheet tints the kept basin and roots ivory and gold.
- **Banners**: two, on gilt poles on the landing's back corners; cloth in `EBHCGalMirr`.
