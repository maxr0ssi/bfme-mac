# Elven forge (`EregionForge`)

Model `EBForge_SKN`, mesh `BOX01`, own texture `ebforgH.tga` (from `ebforge.tga`); new faces sample
the fortress atlas. `Tier.STANDARD`.

EA's furnace tower, arched hearth, weapon rack, stone planter and mallorn stay whole, and so do the
smith, equipment, foliage, upgrade crowns, bones, hearth fire and smoke.

## What changed

- **Chimney**: a silver-rimmed hexagonal collar and a gilt leaf coronet; the flue stays open.
- **Furnace shaft**: six silver-framed lancet windows.
- **Hearth**: a silver barge board and a gilt leaf finial over EA's arch.
- **Lanterns**: three crystal lanterns on the planter, three hanging from the mallorn at EA's
  lantern sites.
- **Weapon rack**: gilt leaf finials on the upturned beam ends.
- **Paint**: mask hints keep the bark and dark steel and turn EA's copper band into silver
  knotwork on enamel.
- **Night**: 16 lights: the tree and planter lanterns, the six shaft windows and the hearth's back
  wall; free glow cards at EA's tree-lantern sites.
- **Banners**: two, one on the chimney, one beside the hearth; cloth in `EBHCForge`.
