## What changed

  crest shows against it.
- **Gable**: a raised, mitred coping up every step; the seven gilt stars of Elendil over the
  niche; a stone acroterion and a steel-and-gilt finial on the apex.
- **Vault**: steel ribs over the slate, a crest of steel spikes along the ridge, a lantern flèche
  (arcaded lantern, slate spire, gilt orb, spike).
- **Banners**: none.
