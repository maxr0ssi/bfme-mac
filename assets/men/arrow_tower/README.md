# Men arrow tower (`MenArrowTowerExpansion`, variation one)

Model `GBFARTOWA`, shipped as our own `GBFARTOWA2` (Blue Mountains draws `GBFARTOWA`), own texture
`GBFortressB.tga` (from `GBFortress1.tga`). `Tier.STANDARD`. Crown from `spire.py`, shared with
[`arrow_tower_b`](../arrow_tower_b/README.md).

## What changed

- **Gallery** round the shaft top on the three outward faces: corbels, a black band of silver stars,
  square merlons.
- **Bartizans** with slate spirelets on the two front corners, on the buttresses' heads.
- **Belfry and dome**: pilasters up the chamfers, pinnacles on the eave, steel ribs, a lantern, gilt
  orb and spike (z 109.0). A moulded plinth at the foot.
- **Banners**: one, on the -Y face; cloth in `GBHCFARTOWA2`.

## Status

Installed with the Men pack. 218 -> 2,612 triangles, height 93.6 -> 109.0 (+16.4 %), 82/82 checks,
`footprint_margin = 0.3` (the dome's eave lies on EA's footprint at -X). Construction, really
damaged and rubble are rebuilt along EA's pieces.
