# Men wall trebuchet (`MenWallTrebuchetSmall`, `ArnorWallTrebuchetSmall`)

Model `GBWallTrebN`, mesh `GBFTRTOWA`. Own texture `GBFortressF.tga` from `GBFortress1.tga`.

## Design (2026-09-27)

EA's ten-sided bastion kept whole (drum, flare, parapet ring with its ironwork, painted arcade),
the platform P1 (the trebuchet's bone) left clear. On the rim, laid on a `dome.Outline` of EA's
outline: a moulded coping, square merlons, a pinnacle with a steel orb on each of the ten corners,
a black band of silver stars round the parapet ring; a White Tree shield on each long face and
arrow slits (wall.py's) on every face.

Corbels under EA's flare were tried and removed: they stood in front of EA's painted arcade and
read as clutter.

No banners. Footprint EA's (x +-26.62, y +-22); height 56 -> 64.47 (+15.1 %).
