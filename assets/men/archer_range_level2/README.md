# Men archer range, level 2 (`V1` of `GBArcheryN_SKN`)

The thickened yard walls and the eight obelisks EA shows from `Upgrade_GondorArcheryRangeLevel2`,
chained on the finished archer range (`base = "men/archer_range"`). Rebuild after it; rebuild
`archer_range_level3` after this. Own texture `GBVA1` (damaged `GBVA1D`, snow `GBVA1_snow`).

## What changed

- **Walls**: a machicolated gallery on the west and east walls' outer faces (corbels, a sable
  slab of silver stars, merlons), merlons on the north wall; both stop short of level 3's
  north-west turret.
- **Obelisks**: a moulded course on the base, a White Tree roundel on the outward face, gilt
  seven-pointed stars on all four faces, a steel collar and a short steel mast with a gilt orb
  and spike (under V1's height limit, 23.86).
- No cloth, no night lights (a level mesh); closed solids (`motifs.closed`).
