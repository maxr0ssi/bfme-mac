# Goblin mine shaft (`WildMineShaft`)

Model `WBPit_SKN`, mesh `WBPITMETAL`, own texture `wbpitH.tga` (from `wbpit2.tga`). Palette E.
EA's collar round the hole, the rock slab, the white rubble and the two torch poles are kept
whole, on EA's stone mound (`WBPITA`). The idea: a working Goblin mine. The v1 ring of fangs is
gone (Max: it read as dentures).

## What changed

- **Winch tower** (the silhouette): four lashed posts on the collar's +X+Y side, girts and X
  braces, a plank deck with a stake parapet, a troll skull on its corner; a boom out over the
  hole's edge with a big iron-rimmed spoked wheel at its end, the chain down to an iron-banded
  bucket, a windlass at the tower's foot winding the rope, a fire basket on the tower.
- **Landing**: a plank landing on two stringers from the collar out beside the bucket.
- **Shoring**: plank revetments bound with iron straps round the collar's inner lip, raking
  timber props with iron shoes and caps on its outer slope, fat rivets on the crest (the collar
  is painted silver iron, Max's pick).
- **Spoil**: a spoil heap and an ore heap of black rock, the ore cart between them.
- **Fire**: iron baskets on EA's two torch poles, where EA's night glows burn.
- **Accents**: two goblin skulls on stakes at the front; one ragged house-colour banner on a post
  with an iron spike (cap 1).

## Kept clear

- The hole's centre and the goblin's climb out of it (EA's idle animations: from (20.2, -1.4,
  -14.7) up to (5.5, -11, 13)); the bucket hangs 15 units off the centre, over the hole's edge.
- The archer's box (x -12..-2, y -37..-16, z 6..22).
- Level 2's six timber frames round the collar (V1) and level 3's rock and tower on the -X side
  (V2, V2A); the winch tower stands between the 5 and 65 degree frames.
